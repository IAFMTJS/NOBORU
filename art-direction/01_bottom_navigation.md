# 01 - Bottom Navigation Concepts

Source: `D:/NOBORU ART/Mockup (1).PNG`

The navbar sheet explores 10 bottom navigation bars. Each bar is a long rounded capsule, usually with Noboru integrated on the left and five navigation items across the bar. The concepts are not just color swaps: each one has its own regional material, decorative edge treatment, active glow, and companion pose.

## Shared Navbar Anatomy

- Shape: wide horizontal rounded capsule, pill-like with heavy radius and slightly flattened center.
- Height: about one fifth to one quarter of a phone screen height when isolated on the concept board; in-app use should be shorter but keep the same proportions.
- Items: 5 evenly spaced actions.
- Standard labels across concepts: `Camp`, `Journey`, `Dojo`, `World`, `Profile`.
- Alternate app nav in later mockups: `Journey`, `Camp`, `Study`, `Bag`, `Profile`.
- Left character area: Noboru overlaps the nav shell, creating a premium illustrated tab bar rather than a plain system nav.
- Active item has three signals:
  - Icon is brighter and colorized.
  - Label uses the same active hue.
  - A glowing dot/underline/pool of light sits beneath the label.
- Inactive items are muted grey, brown, or desaturated black depending on the material.
- Subtle vertical separators appear between nav items in some styles, especially parchment and black panel variants.
- Background behind the nav should be dark enough that the capsule reads clearly.

## Icon Set

### Camp

- Primary symbol: triangular tent/campfire mark.
- Looks like a stylized mountain tent with a small flame or negative-space opening.
- Active camp often glows amber, red, or orange.

### Journey

- Primary symbol: mountain peaks.
- Can be snowy, grey stone, blue ice, or gold depending on theme.
- Active journey glows blue or gold.

### Dojo

- Primary symbol: torii gate.
- Wide top beam, two vertical pillars, center negative space.
- Active dojo glows green, amber, or gold.

### World

- Primary symbol: pagoda/temple or compass.
- Pagoda has stacked roof tiers and small base.
- Cosmic variant uses a compass rose with ring.

### Profile

- Primary symbol: fox head in some concepts, person silhouette in others.
- Fox-head version feels more branded and whimsical.
- Person silhouette feels more conventional and is used in later app screens.

## Implementation Notes For Cursor

- Do not make nav bars flat CSS-only surfaces if generating final art. Each nav should include illustrated material texture and environmental props.
- When using in app UI, keep labels readable even over painterly texture.
- The active underline should be narrow and magical, not a thick modern tab indicator.
- Noboru can overlap the nav bounds by 10-30 percent vertically; this gives the bar personality.
- If a navbar is used inside a full phone screen, reduce illustration density so it does not compete with the main content.
- Use the app-wide icon meanings consistently even when the material style changes.
