# 00 - Global Visual System

## Product Feeling

Noboru should feel like a cozy but epic Japanese mountain-learning adventure. The user is climbing through regions by completing lessons. The app is a language-learning tool, but the visual world should read like a premium mobile RPG: painterly backgrounds, glowing trail nodes, shrines, lanterns, forest paths, foggy peaks, and a loyal fox companion who reacts to progress.

The dominant mood is night camp warmth against deep mountain darkness. The emotional range moves from safe campfire comfort to mysterious locked paths, sakura festival celebration, snowy mountain quiet, and volcanic boss challenge intensity.

## Core Visual Keywords

- Painterly fantasy mobile RPG.
- Japanese mountain pilgrimage.
- Cozy campfire learning.
- Dark forest and moonlit path.
- Shrine, torii, pagoda, rope, lantern, paper charm.
- White fox companion with red markings and scarf.
- Warm amber UI glow over cool blue-black landscapes.
- Hand-crafted wood, parchment, lacquer, stone, brass, snow, bamboo, sakura.
- Progress path as magical illuminated stepping stones.

## Lighting Model

Every scene should have a strong local light source. Avoid evenly lit UI.

- Camp screens: campfire from bottom center, orange light on Noboru's belly and scarf, lantern pools at left/right.
- Trail screens: glowing path nodes create a dotted amber river through the terrain.
- Locked/future areas: fog, desaturation, reduced contrast, grey-blue moonlight, only lock nodes glowing faintly.
- Rewards: central golden halo, sparks, ring particles, star flecks.
- Boss challenge: red underlighting, smoky vignette, ember particles, high contrast.
- Snow: cold blue ambient light, soft white snow highlights, little warm lantern accents.
- Sakura: pale pink daylight/twilight with drifting petals and soft red UI accents.
- Bamboo/dojo: green filtered forest light plus a lime glow under the selected torii icon.

## Composition Rules

- Mobile screens are vertical cards with rounded corners, usually around a 9:16 phone ratio.
- Main content uses deep background art behind transparent UI layers.
- The path often starts near the bottom edge and winds upward into the distance.
- Foreground props create depth: lanterns, stones, grass, flowers, snowbanks, rope, scrolls, camp gear.
- Noboru often anchors the lower left or center foreground, looking toward the active content.
- Important UI panels float over art but never fully erase it; panels are translucent, smoky, or material-based.
- Major completion/reward states use a centered icon with a circular halo.
- Locked nodes are visibly ahead on the path but partially hidden by fog, grayscale, or dim overlays.

## Noboru Character Reference

Noboru is the mascot and emotional anchor.

### Base Design

- Small white fox with oversized triangular ears.
- Soft fluffy cheeks, chest, tail, and ear fur.
- Red-orange shrine-like forehead markings: three angular strokes or flame/leaf shapes.
- Additional small red cheek/eye markings.
- Warm expressive amber eyes, often closed when smiling or meditating.
- Red scarf tied around neck, usually fluttering or folded.
- Adventurer backpack with rolled bedding, straps, buckles, and small hanging charms.
- Paws are rounded and soft, with tiny dark pads when visible.
- Tail is large, fluffy, curled around body when sitting.

### Common Poses

- Sitting at campfire, calm, attentive.
- Walking with backpack and staff on snowy path.
- Reading a book in a parchment/rope nav style.
- Meditating with paws together in bamboo dojo.
- Looking through telescope in cosmic/world style.
- Running with scarf trailing in ember camp style.
- Cheering, proud, worried, surprised, tired, or encouraging inside reaction cards.
- Standing at shrine/checkpoint, small compared with torii gate.
- Peeking from bottom/right of locked detail or empty state cards.

### Expression States

- Teaching: open attentive eyes, gentle smile, paw raised or near book.
- Happy: closed smiling eyes, relaxed ears.
- Proud: upright posture, confident smile, chest forward.
- Worried: ears slightly down, eyebrows lifted, paws close to chest.
- Excited: wide eyes, open mouth, sparks/fireflies around.
- Oops/wrong: embarrassed, head down, dimmer light.
- Out of hearts: sad, tired, small body posture.
- Mastery: centered, glowing golden circle, star particles, serene joy.

## Environmental Motifs

### Mountain Trail

- Winding stone path with many circular glowing nodes.
- Steps and stones are uneven, hand-painted, and wet/shiny in places.
- Tiny houses, shrine lamps, torii gates, and forest silhouettes appear along the path.
- Path is readable as progress: past nodes glow more, future nodes fade or lock.
- Regions include Foot Hills, Forest Trail, Temple Peak, and The Summit.

### Camp

- Tent, campfire, lanterns, logs, backpack, torii gate in background, starry sky.
- Warm fire at center/bottom; UI overlays should feel like wooden camp signs or dark glass.
- Daily quests use a suspended wooden board with rope and carved edges.

### Shrine

- Red torii gate, thick shimenawa rope, hanging paper shide, stone steps, lanterns.
- Achievement/checkpoint screens use shrine architecture as the main frame.
- Rewards sit on a dark rounded panel at bottom with item icons in separate columns.

### Sakura Event

- Pink blossoms framing the trail.
- Petals drifting over UI and nav bars.
- Soft cream/pink surfaces with red progress accents.
- Event cards show festival title, countdown, progress bar, and rewards.

### Bamboo Forest / Dojo

- Green bamboo stalks behind the nav or trail.
- Mossy stones, muted forest shadows, lime glow under active torii.
- Dojo icon is a torii gate rather than a martial arts hall.

### Snow/Ice

- Blue parchment/ice nav base, snowbanks in corners, small stone lantern.
- Noboru wears a blue cloak/scarf and carries a staff.
- Active journey icon glows rich blue.

### Boss / Volcanic Peak

- Red-black atmosphere, smoke, embers, pagoda/temple silhouette.
- Boss node is circular red with oni/fox-mask face or warning icon.
- UI panels are dark with red/orange outlines and very sparse warmth.

## UI Materials

### Dark Glass / Lacquer

- Nearly black translucent panels with subtle vertical gradient.
- Thin border in muted gold, blue-grey, or dark brown.
- Inner shadow and soft outer glow.
- Background art remains visible but dimmed.

### Parchment

- Cream or tan base with fiber texture, stains, creases, and edge darkening.
- Used for light navbars, memory book, scroll panels.
- Borders can be rope, leather cord, stitched trim, or dark brown linework.

### Wood

- Used for daily quest boards and camp signs.
- Horizontal plank texture, carved edges, rope suspension, nail heads.
- Warm brown with bright amber highlights.

### Stone / Ice

- Used for snow navigation and some trail nodes.
- Chipped edges, frost, cold blue shadows, white rim highlights.

### Brass / Gold

- Used sparingly for premium frames, shrine medallions, currency, profile active state.
- Slightly tarnished, not flat yellow.

## Typography Direction

- Use clean readable sans-serif for UI labels.
- Use warm off-white text on dark backgrounds.
- Use orange/gold uppercase small headings for concept labels and section headers.
- Screen titles are modest, not huge marketing headlines.
- Important numbers can be bolder and brighter: XP, streak days, level, progress percentage.
- Button labels are centered and high contrast.
- Japanese learning text can be larger and more calligraphic, especially kanji writing cards.

## Icon Language

Icons must be simple and immediately readable at bottom-nav size, then textured/glowing.

- Camp: triangular tent/campfire/mountain shelter mark.
- Journey: mountain peaks or winding path mark.
- Dojo: torii gate.
- World: pagoda, temple, or compass depending on context.
- Profile: fox head or person silhouette depending on nav style.
- Study: open book.
- Bag: backpack or locked bag.
- Settings: sliders, bell, globe, user, info.
- Lock: rounded padlock with glowing keyhole.
- XP: small badge or rounded square with `XP`.
- Streak: flame icon.
- Gems: faceted violet crystal.
- Currency: coin with warm amber rim.
- Lantern: hanging Japanese lantern with warm core.

## Buttons And Controls

- Primary CTA: red-orange rounded rectangle, darkened top edge, bright inner glow, thin border.
- Secondary button: charcoal/wood/panel surface with subtle border.
- Icon button: small circular or rounded square dark panel, amber/cream icon.
- Tabs: segmented top row with active pill or underline, dark inactive panels.
- Sliders/progress: thin amber/red line with filled segment, sometimes with small circular milestone beads.
- Close button: small `x` at top right, warm grey or gold.
- Back button: left chevron at top left, minimal and warm.

## Progress States

- Incomplete: dim grey node, faint ring, low contrast.
- Current: larger node, orange/gold halo, vertical beam or pulse.
- Completed: green check mark or filled amber node.
- Locked: padlock in grey circle, fog, reduced saturation.
- Boss locked: red node, dark smoke, huge ominous shrine.
- Event node: pink blossom ornament plus red/pink trail line.

## Screen Chrome

- Phone screens use rounded corners around 14-24 px.
- Outer border is usually 1 px muted warm/cool line.
- Bottom nav always sits inside screen, with 5 items.
- UI should leave breathing room from device edges.
- Background art often bleeds to the rounded border; content panels overlay it.

## Particle And Atmosphere Library

- Fire sparks near campfire, rewards, correct answers.
- Drifting sakura petals for event paths and pink navbars.
- Snow particles for snow states.
- Rain streaks and wet glints for rainy weather.
- Dust motes and floating embers for shrine/camp.
- Fog banks over locked future regions.
- Magic rings around mastery, lesson complete, and node activation.

## Do Not Do

- Do not make flat monochrome dashboards.
- Do not use generic stock nature backgrounds without Noboru-specific path, shrine, and lantern details.
- Do not make icons thin outline-only at final size; they need bold silhouettes.
- Do not use pure white backgrounds except small text/glyph areas.
- Do not make empty states blank.
- Do not replace Noboru with a generic cat/dog/mascot.
- Do not remove the Japanese mountain shrine identity.
