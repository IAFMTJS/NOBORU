# 04 - Camp, Inventory, Memory, Events, Shrines

Source: `D:/NOBORU ART/Mockup (4).PNG`

This board expands the world beyond the lesson path. It defines achievement shrines, daily quests, region selection, lesson completion, checkpoints, companion/environmental reactions, inventory, long scrolling trail, memory book, and seasonal event map.

## Screen 1 - Achievement Shrine

Purpose: show achievements and milestones as sacred progress.

Visual details:

- Full vertical phone screen with dark forest shrine background.
- Large red torii gate at top, with thick rope and hanging paper shide.
- Header panel/title: `Achievements`.
- Central hanging medallion/icon with glowing mountain symbol.
- Main achievement: `Mountain Climber`, subtitle `Complete 100 lessons`.
- Noboru sits at lower-left near lanterns and stone steps.
- Milestones row:
  - `10 Lessons`.
  - `50 Lessons`.
  - `100 Lessons` highlighted.
- Badges row:
  - mountain badge.
  - torii badge.
  - fox badge.
  - fox/companion badge.
- Bottom progress pill: `Total Achievements`, `12 / 48`, chevron.

Art direction:

- Shrine architecture should frame the achievement, not sit as background noise.
- Rope, shide, wood grain, lanterns, and stone steps are required.
- Active milestone uses a gold ring and stronger glow.

- Streak panel lower-right: `Daily Streak`, `12 days`, flame icon, milestone dots `10`, `20`, `30`, `50`.

- Wooden board should look suspended or mounted with ropes.
- Progress bars are thin amber fills.

## Screen 6 - Fox Companion Reactions

Purpose: broader Noboru emotion library.

Reaction cards:

- Happy: `Great job! Keep going!`; Noboru smiling, upright, warm light.
- Excited: `Wow! That's amazing!`; Noboru with bright eyes and sparks.
- Encouraging: `You can do it!`; Noboru focused, supportive stance.
- Oops: `Don't worry. Try again!`; Noboru looks apologetic/sad, ears down.
- Proud: `You're incredible!`; Noboru confident, chest forward.

Environmental reactions:

- Sunny: Noboru outdoors in warm sunlight.
- Rainy: Noboru under umbrella, wet blue-grey atmosphere.
- Night: Noboru by lantern/fire in dark blue scene.
- Snowy: Noboru in scarf/cloak with snow falling.

Art direction:

- Reaction state should be readable from Noboru's ears, eyes, mouth, and pose.
- Environmental variants preserve Noboru but change clothing/light/particles.

## Object And Prop Library

### Camp Props

- Canvas tent.
- Campfire logs and stones.
- Lanterns on ground and hanging.
- Backpack, bedroll, rope.
- Wooden signs and planks.
- Small cooking/travel items.

### Shrine Props

- Red torii gates.
- Shimenawa rope.
- White paper shide.
- Stone steps.
- Shrine lanterns.
- Hanging medallions.
- Offering/achievement plaques.

### Inventory Items

- Lantern: warm glowing core, brass frame, small handle.
- Dango: three pastel rice dumplings on skewer.
- Onigiri: white rice triangle with seaweed.
- Daruma: red round doll with face.
- Scroll: rolled parchment with red tie or stamp.
- Omamori: red/gold fabric charm.
- Sakura: pink blossom cluster/petal charm.
- Fan: folded tan fan.
- Fox mask: white mask with red markings.
- Stone lantern/statue: small grey shrine lantern.

### Reward Items

- XP badge: warm rounded badge with `XP`.
- Gems: faceted purple crystal with white highlights.
- Lantern reward: small bright lantern icon.
- Badges: circular gold/bronze medallions with mountain, torii, fox.

## Camp And Shrine Acceptance Criteria

- Camp screens must feel warm and safe; shrine screens must feel ceremonial and earned.
- Quest/reward panels must look like physical objects or premium dark glass, not default cards.
- Inventory items must be individually recognizable by silhouette and material.
- Memory book must look tactile enough to touch: pages, rings, tabs, stains.
- Seasonal events need a visual route, event card, progress, and rewards.
