NOBORU JLPT CONTENT ARCHITECTURE

Version: 1.0

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

This document defines the complete educational architecture of Noboru.

All lessons, vocabulary, grammar, kanji, reading content, listening content, games, reviews, challenges, and progression systems must follow this document.

No educational content may be created outside this structure.

This document is the single source of truth for educational progression.

⸻

EDUCATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

The purpose of Noboru is Japanese mastery.

Not streaks.

Not engagement.

Not content consumption.

Mastery.

Every system must contribute to actual language acquisition.

⸻

EDUCATIONAL MODEL

All learning follows:

Exposure

↓

Understanding

↓

Recognition

↓

Recall

↓

Production

↓

Mastery

↓

Long-Term Retention

Runtime lessons also follow the redesign loop:

Teach → Practice → Combine → Master → Review → Apply

See [learning-system-redesign.md](./learning-system-redesign.md).

No content may skip stages.
