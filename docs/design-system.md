NOBORU DESIGN SYSTEM

Version: 1.0

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

This document defines the visual foundation of Noboru.

Every screen, component, animation, illustration, icon, asset, game, achievement, and interaction must follow this system.

No visual decision may contradict this document.

⸻

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Noboru is not a productivity application.

Noboru is not a corporate learning platform.

Noboru is an adventure toward mastery.

The design should communicate:

* Progress
* Exploration
* Achievement
* Discovery
* Calm Focus
* Premium Quality

⸻

Canonical Yama does not include backpack or temple bell.

Those accessories belong to future variants only.

Do not add them to the default Yama without Art Director approval.
