NOBORU MASCOT EXPANSION SYSTEM

Purpose

The Noboru Mountain Fox should evolve from a static mascot into a living companion.

The mascot should accompany the learner throughout their entire journey.

The fox should feel like a guide, mentor, training partner, and travel companion.

The goal is for users to build an emotional connection with the Mountain Fox.

⸻

Core Philosophy

Most educational mascots are decorative.

The Noboru Mountain Fox should be interactive.

The fox should react to:

* Success
* Failure
* Streaks
* Checkpoints
* Region completions
* Milestones
* Daily training
* Exams
* Achievements

The fox should feel alive.

⸻

Art Requirements

Generate a very large mascot asset library.

Every asset must:

* Have a transparent background
* Be PNG format
* Match existing Noboru art style
* Maintain identical character proportions
* Maintain identical colors
* Maintain identical clothing and accessories
* Maintain identical silhouette

Only facial expressions and poses should change.

The mascot must always remain recognizable.
