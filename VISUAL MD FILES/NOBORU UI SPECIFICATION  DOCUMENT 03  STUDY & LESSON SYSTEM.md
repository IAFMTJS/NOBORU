NOBORU UI SPECIFICATION

DOCUMENT 03

STUDY & LESSON SYSTEM

Version 1.0

⸻

PURPOSE

This document defines every lesson experience inside Noboru.

The purpose of lessons is not merely to test knowledge.

The purpose of lessons is to create the feeling of progressing through a journey.

Every lesson contributes to movement through the mountain world.

The lesson experience must feel rewarding.

Never stressful.

Never overwhelming.

Never corporate.

Never academic.

The player should feel challenged but encouraged.

⸻

LESSON PHILOSOPHY

The player enters lessons frequently.

Many times per session.

Because of this:

clarity is critical

speed is critical

feedback is critical

Every lesson screen should be instantly understandable.

The player should never spend mental energy understanding the interface.

Mental energy should be spent learning.

LESSON ENVIRONMENT

Lessons do not exist in a void.

Lessons remain connected to the world.

Backgrounds should use existing environment artwork.

The player should feel they are studying while traveling.

Not while sitting inside an application.

⸻

LESSON HEADER

The header appears on all lesson screens.

Contains:

Back Button

Lesson Progress

Hearts

Optional Timer

⸻

BACK BUTTON

Top left.

Always visible.

Always accessible.

Never hidden.

⸻

PROGRESS BAR

Position:

Top center.

Purpose:

Show progress through current lesson.

The bar must feel integrated into the visual theme.

Avoid generic mobile progress bars.

Use themed styling.

⸻

HEART SYSTEM

Position:

Top right.

Represents mistakes remaining.

Always visible.

The player should instantly understand risk level.

⸻

LESSON FLOW

Every lesson follows:

Question

Answer

Feedback

Reward

Next Question

No unnecessary screens between questions.

No extra confirmations.

No interruptions.

Teach word recognition.

⸻

VOCABULARY SCREEN LAYOUT

⸻

WORD DISPLAY

Largest element on screen.

Immediate focus point.

No competing visual elements.

Japanese text must always dominate the screen.

⸻

AUDIO BUTTON

Position:

Directly below word.

Purpose:

Play pronunciation.

Large tap target.

Must feel satisfying.

⸻

ANSWER OPTIONS

Position:

Lower third.

Minimum:

2 options.

Maximum:

6 options.

Options should never cover world artwork.

⸻

CORRECT ANSWER FEEDBACK

When correct:

Option highlights.

Gold pulse appears.

Subtle celebration animation.

Fox reaction may appear.

Positive sound plays.

Screen advances automatically.

⸻

INCORRECT ANSWER FEEDBACK

When incorrect:

Wrong answer shakes slightly.

Correct answer reveals.

Heart decreases.

Player receives explanation.

No punishment animation.

No humiliation.

The player should feel guided.

ACCESSIBILITY

Text must remain readable.

Tap targets must remain large.

Color should never be the only indicator.

All important feedback must have motion and icon support.

⸻

FORBIDDEN DESIGNS

Do not use:

white flash screens

generic quiz layouts

corporate forms

survey interfaces

multiple stacked cards

dashboard widgets

spreadsheet-like review tables

generic educational software layouts

Noboru is a journey game that teaches Japanese.

Not an educational website with game elements.
