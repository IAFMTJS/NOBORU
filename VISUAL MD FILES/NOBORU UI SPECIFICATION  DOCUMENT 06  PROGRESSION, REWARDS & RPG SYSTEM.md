NOBORU UI SPECIFICATION

DOCUMENT 06

PROGRESSION, REWARDS & RPG SYSTEM

Version 1.0

⸻

PURPOSE

This document defines:

* progression
* experience points
* levels
* milestones
* rewards
* achievements
* titles
* unlockables
* prestige systems
* progression presentation

Progression is one of the primary motivational systems of Noboru.

Progression must feel meaningful.

Progression must feel earned.

Progression must never feel artificial.

⸻

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Progression is not a number.

Progression is not XP.

Progression is not levels.

Progression is growth.

The player should feel stronger.

The player should feel wiser.

The player should feel further along the mountain.

Every progression system must reinforce this idea.

⸻

PROGRESSION PILLARS

All progression belongs to one of four categories.

Knowledge

Journey

Collection

Mastery

⸻

KNOWLEDGE PROGRESSION

Represents learning.

Examples:

Vocabulary Learned

Kanji Learned

Grammar Learned

Review Accuracy

Mastered Concepts

⸻

JOURNEY PROGRESSION

Represents physical advancement through the world.

Examples:

Trail Completion

Region Completion

Shrine Discovery

Boss Victories

Summit Progress

⸻

COLLECTION PROGRESSION

Represents accumulation.

Examples:

Cosmetics

Decorations

Titles

Achievements

Camp Items

Companion Items

⸻

MASTERY PROGRESSION

Represents expertise.

Examples:

Perfect Reviews

Long Streaks

Advanced Regions

High Accuracy

Rare Accomplishments

⸻

XP SYSTEM

XP represents accumulated effort.

XP should be earned frequently.

XP should feel rewarding.

XP should not dominate the experience.

The player should care more about the journey than the number.

⸻

XP SOURCES

Lesson Completion

Correct Answers

Daily Quests

Achievements

Boss Victories

Review Sessions

Special Events

⸻

XP PRESENTATION

XP appears immediately after earning.

XP presentation should be brief.

XP presentation should never interrupt gameplay.

XP should feel satisfying.

Not intrusive.

⸻

XP VISUALS

Small XP Gain

Subtle floating number.

⸻

Medium XP Gain

Enhanced particle effect.

⸻

Large XP Gain

Celebration effect.

⸻

LEVEL SYSTEM

Levels represent overall progression.

Levels should be easy to understand.

Levels should feel valuable.

Every level matters.

No meaningless filler levels.

⸻

LEVEL PRESENTATION

Player level appears in:

Journey

Camp

Profile

Reward Screens

Leaderboard Screens

⸻

LEVEL UP EXPERIENCE

Level ups should feel special.

The player should immediately recognize:

Something important happened.

⸻

LEVEL UP FLOW

XP Threshold Reached

Level Up Trigger

Celebration Animation

Reward Presentation

Return To Previous Activity

⸻

LEVEL UP VISUAL PRIORITY

Level Up is one of the strongest visual events in the application.

Only major achievements should exceed it.

⸻

TITLES

Titles represent reputation.

Titles are visible prestige markers.

Titles should feel collectible.

Titles should feel meaningful.

⸻

TITLE EXAMPLES

Pathfinder

Wanderer

Scholar

Temple Walker

Mountain Seeker

Lantern Keeper

Master Linguist

Summit Ascendant

⸻

TITLE EQUIPPING

Only one title may be active.

The active title appears:

Profile

Friends

Leaderboards

Achievement Screens

⸻

ACHIEVEMENTS

Achievements celebrate milestones.

Achievements should reward effort.

Achievements should encourage exploration.

Achievements should not encourage unhealthy behavior.

⸻

ACHIEVEMENT CATEGORIES

Learning

Journey

Collection

Mastery

Events

Companion

Social

⸻

ACHIEVEMENT REVEAL

Achievements should feel ceremonial.

Not transactional.

The player should feel recognized.

⸻

ACHIEVEMENT VISUALS

Achievement appears.

Icon revealed.

Title revealed.

Reward shown.

Completion acknowledged.

⸻

HIDDEN ACHIEVEMENTS

Some achievements remain hidden.

Hidden achievements create curiosity.

The player should not see full requirements beforehand.

⸻

MILESTONES

Milestones are larger than achievements.

Milestones represent major life-cycle progression.

Examples:

First Region Completed

100 Lessons Completed

365 Day Streak

First Boss Victory

Summit Reached

⸻

MILESTONE PRESENTATION

Milestones receive dedicated celebration screens.

Milestones should feel memorable.

Players should remember earning them.

⸻

REGION UNLOCKS

Regions are major progression rewards.

Unlocking a new region is one of the most important moments in Noboru.

⸻

REGION UNLOCK SEQUENCE

Current Region Completed

Transition Animation

Gate Opens

Region Revealed

Fox Reacts

Journey Updates

Player Continues

⸻

REWARD PHILOSOPHY

Every reward should have purpose.

Every reward should create anticipation.

Every reward should create ownership.

⸻

REWARD TYPES

XP

Cosmetics

Camp Decorations

Companion Cosmetics

Titles

Badges

Special Effects

Profile Elements

Event Currency

⸻

COSMETIC SYSTEM

Cosmetics represent personal expression.

Cosmetics should never impact learning effectiveness.

No pay-to-win mechanics.

No gameplay advantages.

⸻

CAMP REWARDS

Camp rewards should physically appear within the Camp.

The Camp should visibly evolve.

The player’s home should tell their story.

⸻

JOURNEY REWARDS

Journey rewards affect the trail experience.

Examples:

Lantern Variations

Path Decorations

Special Shrine Visuals

Trail Effects

⸻

COMPANION REWARDS

Companion rewards affect fox presentation.

Examples:

Scarves

Travel Packs

Festival Decorations

Seasonal Items

⸻

COLLECTION SYSTEM

Collected items should be viewable.

Collection screens should feel like museums.

Not inventories.

Not spreadsheets.

⸻

COMPLETION TRACKING

Completion percentages should exist.

Completion percentages should not dominate the UI.

The player should focus on experiences.

Not optimization.

⸻

STREAK SYSTEM

Streaks reward consistency.

Streaks are important.

Streaks should never feel punitive.

⸻

STREAK PHILOSOPHY

Missing a streak should feel disappointing.

Not devastating.

The system should encourage return.

Not guilt.

⸻

STREAK MILESTONES

7 Days

30 Days

100 Days

365 Days

730 Days

1000 Days

⸻

STREAK CELEBRATIONS

Each milestone should receive unique recognition.

Visual variety should exist.

⸻

PRESTIGE SYSTEM

Prestige represents exceptional dedication.

Prestige is optional.

Prestige should never be required.

⸻

PRESTIGE REWARDS

Unique Titles

Rare Cosmetics

Exclusive Camp Decorations

Exclusive Companion Reactions

Special Profile Frames

⸻

PROFILE PRESTIGE

High-level players should be recognizable.

Recognition should feel respectful.

Never flashy.

Never obnoxious.

⸻

RARITY SYSTEM

Items may have rarity.

Rarity should be communicated visually.

⸻

Common

Uncommon

Rare

Epic

Legendary

Mythic

⸻

RARITY PRESENTATION

Higher rarity creates:

better presentation

better reveal effects

better visual treatment

Never stronger gameplay power.

⸻

LEADERBOARD PHILOSOPHY

Leaderboards exist for inspiration.

Not pressure.

Not shame.

Not competition obsession.

⸻

ENDGAME

Endgame players should always have goals.

No player should ever feel finished.

The mountain should always continue.

Mastery should always continue.

Collection should always continue.

Exploration should always continue.

⸻

LONG TERM RETENTION

Players returning after years should still discover:

new achievements

new cosmetics

new events

new collection goals

new companion interactions

⸻

IMMERSION RULES

Rewards should originate from the world.

Achievements should feel discovered.

Progress should feel earned.

Nothing should feel artificially gamified.

The player should feel like a traveler becoming a master.

Not a user completing tasks.

⸻

FORBIDDEN DESIGNS

Do not use:

loot box systems

random gambling mechanics

pay-to-win rewards

artificial progression walls

endless currency inflation

reward spam

notification spam

meaningless badge collections

generic RPG stat sheets

The progression system exists to reinforce the journey.

Not replace it.

⸻

END OF DOCUMENT
